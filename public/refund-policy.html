Refund Policy

Transparency is at the heart of everything we do. Here's what you need to know about payments and refunds on Hive4U.

Last Updated: April 2026

Key Point: Hive4U is a listing and discovery platform — not a booking or payment platform. We do not collect rent, deposits, or any payments from users. All financial transactions occur directly between users and property owners.

1. Hive4U as a Listing Platform

Hive4U operates exclusively as a listing and discovery platform. Our role is to:

  • Curate and display verified accommodation listings (PGs, flats, hostels) across Delhi NCR and other Indian cities.
  • Connect prospective tenants with property owners and managers.
  • Facilitate initial communication through WhatsApp and other channels.

We do not act as a real estate agent, broker, or intermediary in any financial transaction. Hive4U does not charge brokerage, service fees, or commissions to users for finding accommodations.

2. Payments Are Between User and Property Owner

Any and all payments related to accommodation — including but not limited to rent, security deposits, maintenance charges, advance payments, and other fees — are transactions that occur directly between you (the user) and the property owner or manager.

Hive4U:

  • Does not collect, process, or handle any payments on behalf of users or property owners.
  • Does not hold any funds in escrow or as an intermediary.
  • Does not charge any booking fees, platform fees, or commissions to users.
  • Has no control over the payment terms, refund policies, or financial arrangements set by individual property owners.

3. Hive4U Does Not Process Payments

Since Hive4U does not process, collect, or manage any payments, we are unable to issue refunds for any transactions made between users and property owners. This includes:

  • Security deposits paid to property owners.
  • Advance rent or booking amounts.
  • Maintenance or utility charges.
  • Any other payments made directly to a property owner or their representative.

Refund requests, if any, must be directed to the property owner or manager with whom the payment was made.

4. Dispute Resolution Guidance

While Hive4U cannot intervene in financial disputes between users and property owners, we are committed to helping our users and can offer the following guidance:

If You Have a Payment Dispute

  • Contact the Property Owner Directly: Reach out to the property owner or manager to discuss and attempt to resolve the issue amicably.
  • Document Everything: Keep records of all payments, receipts, WhatsApp conversations, agreements, and any other relevant documentation.
  • Reach Out to Hive4U: If you believe a listing was fraudulent or misleading, contact us immediately at hello@hive4u.in. We will investigate the matter and may remove the listing from our platform.
  • Seek Legal Recourse: For unresolved disputes, you may consider approaching your local consumer forum or seeking legal advice under Indian consumer protection laws.

How Hive4U Can Help

Although we cannot process refunds, we can assist in the following ways:

  • Facilitate communication between you and the property owner to help resolve misunderstandings.
  • Investigate complaints about misleading or fraudulent listings and take appropriate action, including removal from the platform.
  • Blacklist property owners who engage in dishonest practices.
  • Provide documentation of listing details as they appeared on our platform at the time of your inquiry.

5. Tips for Safe Transactions

To protect yourself when dealing with property owners, we recommend:

  • Visit the Property in Person: Always visit and inspect the property before making any payment.
  • Verify Ownership: Ask for property ownership documents or authorization letters from the property owner.
  • Get Written Agreements: Ensure all terms — rent amount, deposit, notice period, amenities included — are documented in a written rental agreement.
  • Use Traceable Payment Methods: Avoid cash payments when possible. Use bank transfers, UPI, or cheques that create a payment trail.
  • Request Receipts: Always obtain receipts for every payment you make.
  • Be Cautious of Deals That Seem Too Good: If a listing offers significantly below-market pricing, verify its authenticity before committing.

6. Reporting Fraudulent Listings

If you encounter a listing on Hive4U that you believe is fraudulent, misleading, or inaccurate, please report it to us immediately. We take such reports very seriously and will:

  • Investigate the listing within 48 hours of receiving your report.
  • Contact the property owner for verification.
  • Remove the listing if it is found to be fraudulent or inaccurate.
  • Take measures to prevent similar listings in the future.

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
