Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Hive4U collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Hive4U ("we," "our," or "us"). We operate the website hive4u.in and provide a platform that connects users with PG accommodations, flats, hostels, and other rental listings across Delhi NCR and other cities in India.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with us via WhatsApp or other communication channels. Please read this policy carefully. By using our platform, you consent to the practices described herein.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Contact Information: Name, phone number, and email address when you fill out our contact form or reach out via WhatsApp.
  • Search Preferences: Your accommodation preferences, including location, budget, and property type.
  • Communication Data: Messages exchanged with us through our contact form, email, or WhatsApp.
  • Property Listing Data: If you are a property owner, details about your property including address, amenities, pricing, and photographs.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns, and referring URLs.
  • Location Data: Approximate geographic location based on your IP address.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, and server log information.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly, including theme preferences and session management.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve our services.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings such as language, region, and display preferences.

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To connect you with suitable accommodation listings based on your preferences.
  • To facilitate communication between you and property owners via WhatsApp or other channels.
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To improve and optimize our website, content, and user experience.
  • To send you relevant updates about listings that match your preferences (with your consent).
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent activity and ensure platform security.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service.

5. WhatsApp Communication

Hive4U uses WhatsApp as a primary communication channel for connecting users with property owners and for customer support. When you interact with us on WhatsApp:

  • Your phone number and WhatsApp profile information are visible to us.
  • Messages you send may include personal details such as your name, preferences, and budget.
  • We may share your contact details with verified property owners to facilitate direct communication regarding listings you have expressed interest in.
  • WhatsApp conversations are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy in addition to ours.

We will never sell your WhatsApp contact information to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, services, or platforms (including property owner websites, maps, and social media profiles). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit through links on our platform. Hive4U bears no liability for how third parties handle your data.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With Property Owners: To connect you with accommodation providers you have expressed interest in.
  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business (e.g., analytics, hosting).
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as a business asset.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Secure HTTPS encryption for all website communications.
  • Regular security assessments and updates.
  • Restricted access to personal data on a need-to-know basis.
  • Secure data storage practices.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Objection: Object to the processing of your data for specific purposes.
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service provider.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw previously given consent for data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@hive4u.in. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. When your data is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

11. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover that we have collected data from a person under 18, we will promptly delete that information.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
